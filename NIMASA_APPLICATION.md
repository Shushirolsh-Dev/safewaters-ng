# NIMASA Application Draft - SafeWaters NG

## Problem (150 words)
Over 200,000 artisanal boats operate invisibly to AIS. Capsizes, engine failures and missing boats in Lagos Lagoon and coastal channels go unreported for hours. NIMASA Deep Blue protects large vessels, but small crafts have no low-cost tracker, no SOS channel, no incident map. Result: preventable deaths, lost catch, insurers and banks won't serve fishers without trip data.

## Solution
SafeWaters NG: offline-first PWA. Start Trip -> GPS breadcrumbs stored locally -> one-tap SOS (SMS fallback via Termii) -> community hazard reports -> public incident map + responder dashboard. No hardware. Works on N40k Android. Pidgin/Yoruba voice prompts in v2.

## Innovation
Combines safety + fisheries + ocean data in one trip log. Offline queue + SMS bridge is new for this segment. Every trip builds a data asset banks/insurers can price.

## Business model
Freemium fishers, N500/mo cooperative plan, responder/insurance API, paid SMS alerts. Pilot: 500 fishers Lagos Lagoon.
